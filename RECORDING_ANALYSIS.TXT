ANALYSIS OF STANDARD RECORDING 2.MP3
====================================

The uploaded Bondwell recording is approximately 67.15 seconds long. The
actual audio playback begins after roughly 3.3 seconds and lasts about 63.2
seconds, which closely matches the 63.192-second source. This indicates that
the original player's overall timing was already close to correct.

The harsh character is therefore not primarily a playback-speed problem. The
recording shows strong high-frequency sampling components associated with the
original 2000-writes-per-second staircase output, together with the granular
noise produced by one-bit fixed-step delta modulation.

The replacement BWA4 player addresses both causes by using two-bit
small/large differential codes and four interpolated MC1408 writes per source
sample, raising the DAC update rate to approximately 10 kHz.
